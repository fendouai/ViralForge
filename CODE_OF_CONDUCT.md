# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want ViralForge to be a place where people can learn, build and disagree
without being harassed. We pledge to make participation welcoming regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and generous with the person.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith; ask before escalating.
- Respect privacy and do not share other people's media or identity.
- Credit prior art and respect licenses.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or sexualized attention.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging face swapping, impersonation, or non-consensual media.
- Deliberately bypassing platform terms or the project's safety boundaries.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate
this code. Report concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Reports
will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential.

This is a concise adaptation of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
